Top life insurers: premium growth vs profit margin

Bubble size is gross premium in the end year. Like the Deloitte East Africa report, profit margin stands in for embedded value, which Kenyan insurers do not publish: profit before tax (or the revenue-account surplus where that is all IRA prints) over gross premium, averaged across the window. Years whose printed profit is more than half of premium (a likely filing error, such as Kenindia's 2024 return) are left out of the average and marked *. From 2024 profit is on IFRS 17, so margins before and after are not strictly comparable.
